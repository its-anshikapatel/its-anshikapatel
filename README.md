## 👩💻 About Me

I'm a final-year **B.Tech Computer Science** student specialising in **Cybersecurity** (graduating **June 2027**), and I build **secure-by-design AI systems** — the kind where the guardrails are part of the architecture, not bolted on afterwards.

My work sits at the intersection of three things: **LLM applications that can't be talked into destructive actions**, **low-latency concurrent systems in C++17**, and **data pipelines** that turn messy public data into something a human can actually decide with.

- 🛡️ &nbsp;**Security-first by default** — every LLM project I ship enforces allow-lists, row limits and timeouts at the query layer
- ⚙️ &nbsp;**Systems that hold up under load** — multithreaded C++17 engines with thread-safe queues
- 📊 &nbsp;**Full-stack delivery** — Python/PostgreSQL/Streamlit pipelines through to React and Next.js front-ends
- 🤝 &nbsp;**Team lead** — leading a 6-member team on an AI proctoring MVP, and elected Student Body President twice
- 🇫🇷 &nbsp;**Open to relocation across France** (Paris & all regions) — available for a **stage from 2027**
